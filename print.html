Author's Note

This book is not fiction. Every name, every date, every memory is real. I wrote it for my parents, my sisters, and for the boy I used to be.

Some memories are painful. Some are joyful. All of them are mine.

I have tried to be honest. I have tried to remember things exactly as they happened — though memory, like everything, fades with time.

If you find yourself in these pages, know that you mattered. You still do.

— Ravi Raj Singh

    How to Read This Book

    This is a memoir — a true story of a boy growing up in Begusarai, Bihar. It covers the years 2008 to 2019, across eleven chapters.

    You can read it in order, or begin anywhere. Each chapter is a self-contained memory. The Hinglish edition is available alongside the English.

    The story continues. It is still being written.

    ❦

    Begin anywhere. Read slowly.
    This story belongs to you now.

    The Story Behind the Story

    I didn't plan to write a book. I started writing — one memory at a time. One evening. One cup of chai.

    It began as a diary. Then it became a way to understand myself.

    I wrote about my childhood because I wanted to remember it. I wrote about my struggles because I wanted to survive them. And I wrote about my dreams because I still believe in them.

    This book is not the end. It is the beginning of a longer conversation — with myself, and with you.

    — Ravi Raj Singh

    About the Author

    Ravi Raj Singh

    Author

    Ravi Raj Singh is a writer based in Patna, India. He has kept a personal diary since childhood, and began writing online around 2023–24.

    "A Boy Who Never Thought" is his first book. It grew out of that habit. The book is written in both English and Hinglish, and remains in progress.

    His writing focuses on ordinary lives, small moments, and the quiet ways a person grows.
