*Bandwar, March 2008*

Us raat hospital mein doctor nahi tha. Kuch hafton se nahi tha — ya kuch mahine, kisi ko theek se yaad bhi nahi. Toh Mummy ko andhere kheton ke paar padosi ke ghar le jaana pada, jahan ek chhota sa kamra khaali kar liya gaya tha, aur jahan Begusarai se ek aurat aane ko raazi ho gayi thi.

Kamra chhota tha. Chhat neechi thi. Ek bulb taar se latak raha tha, aur deewar par kisi ne pichle saal ka calendar chipka rakha tha.

Us kamre ke andar, Mummy zameen par bichhe gadde par leti thi, dard se karah rahi thi. Bahar, aangan mein, Papa akele lakdi ke stool par baithe the, intezaar karte hue.

Unhe samajh nahi aa raha tha ki haathon ka kya karein.

---

Jab bachcha paida hua — jab main paida hua — toh Begusarai se aayi aurat ne apne haath kapde se ponche, aur Dadi ko dhoondhne chali gayi. Dadi darwaze ke paas baithi thi, zameen ki taraf dekhte hue.

*"Chachi,"* aurat ne kaha, apni sapaat, khabar sunane wali awaaz mein. *"Pota hua hai."*

Dadi muskurai nahi. Wo royi nahi. Wo bas uthi, padosi ke baramde tak chali, aur Papa ko phone lagaya.

Unhone teen shabd kahe. Unhe isse zyada kehne ki zaroorat nahi thi.

---

Papa us raat gaon mein nahi the. Wo paas ke ek kasbe mein the, kisi rishtedaar ke ghar, kisi aise kaam ke liye jiske baare mein main aaj tak nahi jaan paaya.

Jab phone baja, unhone uthaya.

Suna.

Aur phir chup ho gaye.

Ye sadme ki chuppi nahi thi. Khushi ki chuppi bhi nahi. Bas chuppi. Woh chuppi jisme aadmi tab doobta hai jab use khud nahi pata hota ki use kya mehsoos karna chahiye.

Mummy ne mujhe ye kahani bahut baar sunayi hai. Wo kehti hain ki us raat unhone Papa ka chehra nahi dekha — wo us kamre mein nahi the — par unhone saalon mein use kayi baar apni kalpana mein dekha. Unhone kalpana ki ki kaise unhone phone kaan se lagaya aur kuch nahi kaha. Unhone kalpana ki ki kaise wo us doosre kasbe mein, us doosre ghar mein, kisi aur ki lamp ki roshni mein khade rahe, aur suna ki unka ek beta hua hai.

*"Unhone kya kaha?"* maine ek baar poocha tha.

Mummy ne kuch der socha.

*"Kuch nahi,"* unhone kaha. *"Kuch nahi kaha."*

---

Jab Papa aakhir mein pahunche — us raat der se — toh wo us kamre mein aaye jahan Mummy leti thi aur main unke paas leta tha. Wo bole nahi. Wo roye nahi. Wo bas gadde ke kinaare baith gaye aur der tak mujhe dekhte rahe.

Phir, bahut dheere se, unhone mujhe uthaya aur apne seene se laga liya.

Zyada der ke liye nahi. Ek minute, shayad do. Par Mummy use un dino ki pehli — aur shayad akele — aisi ghadi maanti hain jab unhone khud ko kuch mehsoos karne diya.

Wo chhabbees saal ke the. Unki ek patni thi, do betiyan thi, aur ab ek beta tha. Ek ghar tha jo jald hi batne wala tha. Ek naukri thi jo zaroorat ke hisaab se kam paise deti thi. Us raat unhe nahi pata tha ki ye sab kya banega.

Wo bas mujhe thaame rahe.

Phir unhone mujhe wapas Mummy ko diya, aur bahar jaakar phir us stool par baith gaye, aur aasman ko dekhne lage.

---

Chhe din tak mera koi naam nahi tha.

Ye humare gaon mein asaamaanya nahi hai, par kehne layak hai: ek bachcha ek hafte tak bina naam ke jee sakta hai. Ye koi sankat nahi hai. Ye koi jaldi ki baat bhi nahi hai. Bachcha khayega. Bachcha soyega. Bachcha kayi haathon mein rahega, aur har haath use kuch alag bulayega — ek awaaz, ek komalta, ek shabd.

Un chhe dino mein, main sirf *"naya wala"* tha.

Phir, saatve din, naamkaran hua. Pandit ji subah aaye — patle, shaant aadmi, jinke kapde ke thailay mein unki kitaabein thi. Wo mukhya kamre mein zameen par paalthi maarkar baithe, woh kundli kholi jo Mummy ne taiyaar rakhi thi, aur der tak use padhte rahe.

Phir unhone nazar uthayi.

*"N se rakhna,"* unhone kaha. *"Shubh rahega."*

N akshar se naam.

Kamre mein sujhaav ghoomne lage. Nishant. Chinmay. Naman. Har sujhaav bola gaya, socha gaya, ek taraf rakha gaya.

Mummy chup rahi.

Wo kone mein baithi thi, mujhe god mein liye. Unke baal khule the. Aankhon ke neeche abhi bhi janm ki thakan ke gaddhe the. Wo ek hafte se theek se soyi nahi thi.

Phir wo bolin.

*"R se rakhna hai,"* unhone kaha. *"R se hi hoga."*

Kamra chup ho gaya. Pandit ji ne unhe dekha, phir Papa ko, phir wapas unhe.

*"R?"* unhone kaha.

*"R,"* Mummy ne kaha.

Kisi ne bahas nahi ki. Jab Mummy kuch thaan leti thi, toh koi bahas nahi karta tha. Par kisi ko samajh bhi nahi aaya. R kyun? N mein kya kharabi thi?

Jawab baad mein aaya, jab maine poocha. Saalon baad, jab main itna bada ho gaya ki pooch sakoon.

*"Kyunki mera naam Rajani hai,"* unhone kaha. *"Maine socha — agar bete ka akshar maa jaisa ho, toh achhi baat hai."*

---

Pandit ji us din bina naam diye chale gaye. Unhe overrule kar diya gaya tha, aur wo jaante the, aur unhone is par kuch aur nahi kaha. Mummy ne man bana liya tha. Lekin unhone ye tay nahi kiya tha ki *kaunsa* R.

Ravi? Rahul? Raj? Rohit?

Din beette gaye. Naam nahi aaya.

Aur is beech — naam ki gair-maujoodgi se bani us jagah mein — kuch aur aa gaya.

---

Main mushkil bachcha tha. Mummy ye narmi se kehti hain, par kehti hain. Main raat bhar rota tha. Gala baith jaane tak rota tha. Meri behenein apne kaan takiye se daba leti thi. Papa andhere mein uth baithte the, kuch samajh nahi aata tha.

Phir ek raat, ek padosi — do ghar door rehne wala ek naujawan — ne mujhe sadak se suna. Wo ghar laut rahe the, unhone kaha, aur unhone door se awaaz suni thi, aur unhone socha tha: *ye kya hai?* Aur wo andar aa gaye.

Wo darwaze par khade hokar mujhe dekhte rahe. Phir wo hanse.

*"Arre,"* unhone kaha. *"Ye toh ghantee hai. Bajti rehti hai."*

*Ghantee.*

Chhoti ghantee.

Sab hasne lage. Mummy hansi. Papa ne, kayi dino mein pehli baar, hansi.

Aur us raat se, unhone mujhe ghantee bulana shuru kiya. Mummy ne mujhe ghantee bulaya. Papa ne ghantee bulaya. Dadi ne — jo usi saal chali jaane wali thi — ghantee bulaya. Nani ne, padosiyon ne, poore gaon ne.

Ye naam saalon mere saath raha. Uske baad bhi jab mujhe asli naam mil gaya. Uske baad bhi jab main samajhne laga ki naam ek chhoti cheez hai aur ek badi cheez bhi.

---

Mummy, jab main chhota tha, jaan-boojhkar mujhe darati thi.

Ye likhna ajeeb hai, par sach hai. Jab main bahut rota tha — jab kuch aur kaam nahi karta — toh wo mere oopar khadi hoti, apna chehra bigaadti, apni awaaz neechi karti, aur kehti:

*"Bhoot aa gaya! Bhoot aa gaya!"*

Aur main chup ho jaata. Turant. Mera chhota shareer jam jaata, aankhein phail jaati, aur main unhe aise dekhta jaise wo duniya ki sabse darawni cheez hon.

Jo, us pal mein, wo thi.

Aur phir — kuch second baad, jab chuppi tik jaati — wo mujhe god mein utha leti, doodh pilati, gungunati, sir sehlati.

Dar aur pyaar, ek hi haathon se. Ek hi minute mein.

Maine is par bahut socha hai, bade hokar. Mujhe nahi lagta ki Mummy beraham thi. Mujhe lagta hai ki wo ek jawaan aurat thi, batte hue ghar mein akeli, teen bachchon ke saath, aur ek aise pati ke saath jo unse baat nahi kar paata tha, aur koi madad nahi, aur koi aaram nahi — aur wo wahi kar rahi thi jo unse pehle kayi peedhiyon ki maaon ne kiya tha: jo haath laga, wahi istemaal kiya.

Par mujhe ye bhi lagta hai ki ye pehli cheez hai jo maine pyaar ke baare mein seekhi. Ki wo bhayanak ho sakta hai. Ki wo kisi aur cheez ka roop dhar kar aa sakta hai. Ki wo tumhe chup kara sakta hai, aur phir tumhe thaam sakta hai jab tum rote ho.

Main chhe din ka tha aur mujhe apna naam nahi pata tha. Par mujhe ye pata tha: ki jo insaan mujhe duniya mein sabse zyada darata tha, wahi wo insaan tha jo mujhe jaane nahi dega.

---

Mujhe isme se kuch bhi yaad nahi hai. Main bahut chhota tha. Mujhe baad ki cheezein yaad hain — ghar, khet, meri behenein, Mummy ki awaaz ka lehja — par mujhe Bandwar yaad nahi hai. Mujhe Dadi yaad nahi hain. Mujhe wo kamra yaad nahi hai jahan main paida hua.

Jo mujhe yaad hai, wo wo hai jo mujhe bataya gaya. Aur jo mujhe bataya gaya, mere bachpan ke zyadatar hisse mein, bahut kam tha.

2024 mein — jab main 10th mein tha, solah saal ka, un parikshaon ki taiyari karte hue jo mere agle dashak ka faisla karne wali thi — tab Mummy ne mujhe poori kahani sunayi.

Ek baithak mein nahi. Kabhi ek baithak mein nahi. Tukdon mein, kayi hafton mein, jab mauka sahi hota. Unhone mujhe mere janm ki raat ke baare mein bataya, aur Seema aunty ke ghar ke baare mein, aur Dadi dwara khabar sabse pehle sunne ke baare mein. Unhone bataya chhe din bina naam ke, aur pandit ji, aur R.

Aur unhone ek aur baat batayi, jo mujhe nahi pata thi: ki Dadi ki mrityu tab hui thi jab main gyarah mahine ka tha.

Mujhe ye pata tha, beshak. Mujhe pata tha ki Dadi ki mrityu tab hui thi jab main chhota tha. Par mujhe wo doosri baat nahi pata thi. Wo baat jo mere saath reh gayi.

Ek tasveer hai. Kahin ek purane album mein. Us din ki jab Dadi chal basin.

Tasveer mein, main unke paas leta hoon. Gyarah mahine ka. Sota hua.

Mujhe nahi pata kisne kheechi. Mujhe nahi pata kyun. Par wo hai.

Mujhe wo 2019 mein mili. Main chhati mein tha. Main kisi aur cheez ke liye album dekh raha tha, aur wo wahan thi — mera nanha shareer meri Dadi ke shareer ke paas, aur drishya mein kuch aisa jo main tab samajh nahi paaya.

Maine use der tak dekha.

Mujhe nahi pata tha uska kya karoon.

Toh maine use wapas album mein rakha, album band kiya, aur bahar khelne chala gaya.

Ab, jab main ye likh raha hoon, main samajhta hoon ki main kya dekh raha tha. Meri Dadi, jinhone mere janm ki khabar sabse pehle suni. Meri Dadi, jinhone mujhe naam milne se pehle upnaam diya. Meri Dadi, jo usse chaar mahine pehle chal basin jab main unhe yaad rakh paata.

Tasveer mein, main unke paas so raha hoon, aur wo pehle se thandi hain.

Mujhe nahi pata ki unhone mujhe kabhi god mein liya ya nahi.

Mujhe kabhi pata nahi chalega.

Par mujhe ye pata hai: janm aur naamkaran ke beech ke un chhe dino mein, us benaam, nirakar samay mein — unhone mujhe god mein liya hoga. Mujhe yakeen hai. Jis aurat ne khabar sabse pehle suni, wo mujhe god mein liye bina nahi reh sakti thi.

Yahi maanna maine chuna hai.

---

Mere janm ke do din baad, mera naam aakhirkaar tay hua. Ravi Raj. Ye Mummy ka faisla tha, aur kisi ne us par sawaal nahi uthaya.

Par tab tak, naam der kar chuka tha. Gaon pehle hi tay kar chuka tha ki mujhe kya bulayega.

*Ghantee.*

Chhoti ghantee.
