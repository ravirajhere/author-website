02 / About

About the Author

Ravi Raj Singh is a writer based in Patna, India. He has kept a personal diary since childhood, and began writing online around 2023–24. "A Boy Who Never Thought" — his first book — grew out of that habit.

The book is written in both English and Hinglish, and remains in progress. His writing focuses on ordinary lives, small moments, and the quiet ways a person grows.

03 / The Book

A Boy Who Never Thought

"A Boy Who Never Thought" is a journey still being written — from 2008 to today. Told through the eyes of a boy growing up in a small home in Begusarai, it moves through loss, family division, changing schools, and financial struggle — while a boy keeps walking forward without quite knowing why.

This is not the story of a hero. It is the story of an ordinary child — one who is unhappy with his rank, who dreams of becoming an engineer without knowing what that means, and who slowly begins to understand himself.

Eleven chapters have been written so far, covering 2008 to 2019. The remaining journey will be completed by 2026.
